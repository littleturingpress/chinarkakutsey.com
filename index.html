About me

Hello! I'm a software engineer and a dad. For almost twenty years I have built computer systems that help engineers design cars, machines and factories. Today I run my own software company in Nürnberg, Germany.

At home, my favourite job is making puzzles for my 8-year-old son. It started with secret codes on the breakfast table and treasure hunts around the house. Soon there were binary riddles, logic puzzles and mysteries to solve before bedtime. He always asked for "just one more!", and those puzzles became my first book.

In my job, finding a bug is a lot like solving a mystery: collect the clues, test every idea, and never trust a computer just because it sounds sure. That's what I want children to discover. Computer science is not only about screens. It's about thinking clearly, asking good questions, and having fun with a pencil and paper.
